## ИТОГО A6

- **20 шотов**, ~100 сек
- **4 iconic frames:** SH08 (последний warm leitmotif), SH12 (glasses-chain рифма с матерью), SH14 (подпись — central frame of film), SH20 (leitmotif death visualized as light absence)
- **Leitmotif death sequence:**
  - SH08 — **maximum loud, triple-tone at peak intensity** (warm + cool + steel hatching)
  - SH15 — **final pulse + fade across shot duration** (steel hatching dissolves frame-by-frame)
  - SH18-19 — **no warm anywhere** (cool silver afternoon)
  - SH20 — **complete absence** (pure cool single-tone, no layered lighting at all)
- **Visual rhymes baked into composition (not VO):**
  - SH12 notary glasses-chain ↔ A1_SH04 Galina glasses-chain (identical CU composition)
  - SH16 OTS shot ↔ A1_SH05 OTS through Galina (mirror over time)
- **SingleWithBack applied:** SH01 (heroine PRIMARY/daughter fragment), SH02 (heroine PRIMARY/daughter back), SH06 (heroine PRIMARY/Sveta hand-only), SH13 (heroine PRIMARY/Sveta shoulder/notary hand only), SH16 (notary hand only/heroine back), SH18 (heroine PRIMARY/Sveta back-of-head wrapping)
