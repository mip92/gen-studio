# bio_plus — Shot prompts: CODA «Та самая аптека» (10 шотов)

> Продолжение того же зимнего вечера в общежитии. Heroine 46.
> **Палитра:** `{PALETTE_CODA}` — silvery night blue through dormitory window + distant warm yellow pharmacy sign on horizon.
> **Beat:** Final Image + дисклеймер overlay.
> **Locale:** окно общежития на улице Шмидта 4, точка с видом на аптеку «Здоровье» через двор (12 courtyards distance).
> **Voice constraint:** narrator продолжает в 2-м лице («ты»), не переходит в «я / моя мать». Никаких прямых обращений к зрителю.
> **Leitmotif lighting:** mortuus est — все heroine close-ups в flat cool single-tone. Warm только на горизонте (distant pharmacy).

## ИТОГО Coda

- **10 шотов**, ~50 сек
- **3 iconic frames:** SH04 (mother's cross with horizon-pixel-catch), SH09 (heroine's last sustained close-up), SH10 (final image + disclaimer overlay)
- **Visual signature of Coda:** cool indigo room + distant warm pharmacy rectangle on horizon — the only saturated warm light in the entire act is twelve courtyards away (about 850 metres) — the location where the entire arc began in A1_SH04
- **Leitmotif lighting:** completely dead. Heroine's face renders in flat cool single-tone throughout. The ONLY warm pixels in any frame are: (1) the distant pharmacy sign in the background, (2) a thin warm pixel-line along the cross-tip or cheek contour catching the distant horizon light. The triple-tone glow that accompanied her from A1_SH22 to A6_SH15 never appears here.
- **Voice constraint preserved:** narrator stays in "ты" throughout, no shift to "я", no 4th wall break addressing viewer
- **Disclaimer overlay:** rendered as part of C_SH10 composition — white bold cyrillic over the final image
