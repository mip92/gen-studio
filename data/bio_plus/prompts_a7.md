## ИТОГО A7

- **25 шотов**, ~125 сек
- **8 iconic frames:** SH01 (общежитие), SH04 (крестик-anchor reveal), SH06 (name reveal "Елена"), SH12 (Лариса), SH16 (заколка-стрекоза на полке у дочки), SH22 (Светлана в наручниках), SH24 (вид на аптеку через двор), SH25 (двойной образ — bridge to Coda)
- **Leitmotif lighting:** completely absent through entire act. All Elena close-ups in flat cool single-tone (SH04, SH10, SH17, SH23) or cool two-tone with cool blue TV-cast (SH23). The warm cheekbone glow that accompanied her from A1_SH22 through A6_SH15 is gone forever.
- **Name reveal mechanics:** name "Елена" first appears in shot only on her cleaner's uniform badge (SH06) — written by hand in marker on a white sticker over a printed employee number. The name as label, not identity.
- **Anchor reveals:**
  - Silver cross worn OVER blouse (SH04) — for first time in the film
  - Dragonfly clip on shelf, not in hair (SH16) — older daughter has stopped wearing it, just keeps it
  - Plush rabbit gone (SH19) — younger daughter has discarded it
  - Sveta's gold badge absent (SH22) — broadcasted shame
  - BIO+ turquoise bottles absent from shelves (SH09) — brand dead
- **SingleWithBack applied:** SH12 (Larisa PRIMARY/heroine shoulder), SH16 (Older Daughter on-screen PRIMARY), SH19 (Younger Daughter PRIMARY/heroine shoulder), SH22 (Sveta-on-screen PRIMARY)
