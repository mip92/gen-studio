## ИТОГО A5

- **25 шотов**, ~125 сек
- **3 iconic frames:** SH11 (кухня ночью), SH15 (звонок Светлане в 2 ночи), SH25 (мутация leitmotif)
- **SingleWithBack applied:** SH01 (heroine PRIMARY/Larisa back-of-head), SH02 (Larisa PRIMARY/heroine shoulder), SH06 (Larisa PRIMARY + phone screen), SH16-18/20/22 (Sveta-on-screen PRIMARY)
- **Sveta's only "vulnerable" appearance** — SH16 (and through SH18/20/22 phone-screen variations) — no makeup, no badge, robe. This is the only shot in the film where her corporate armor is off
- **Leitmotif arc in A5:**
  - SH19 — leitmotif **absent** (heroine freezes for 4 seconds — knot disappeared)
  - SH21 — leitmotif **returns mutated** (steel-grey hatching layered over warm-key cheekbone glow)
  - SH25 — **mutation locked in** (triple-layer light, leitmotif now permanently corrupted)
- **Black window** SH11 + SH25 — visual signature of A5 (deepest night, no horizon)
